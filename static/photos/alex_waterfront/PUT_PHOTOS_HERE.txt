Put the client's photos for this map here.

From the client's "all resources files.rar" for this corniche, copy the
CONTENTS of its "images" folder into this folder (or copy the "images"
folder itself in here - both work).

File names can stay exactly as the client exported them, e.g.
  C__Program Files_QGIS 3.22.5_map pictures_<name>.jpg   or   <name>.jpg

After adding photos, restart the app (sudo systemctl restart hcsr-maps on the server).
